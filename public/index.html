About Us

Serving Indian kitchens with taste & health for over five decades.

Kirti Group today is one of India's leading edible oil and high protein de-oiled cake manufacturers. Based in Latur, the agro-industrial hub of Maharashtra, Kirti Gold operates state-of-the-art manufacturing plants configured with modern Belgian technology.

Operated by industry experts and professionals, our mission is driven by a laser-sharp focus on organic crop purchasing, encouraging domestic farming communities, and promoting healthy lifestyles with natural products.

Why Kirti Gold?

Our core commitment is built on non-compromised standards, local sourcing, and futuristic technology.

Sampoorna Swadeshi

We follow the Atma Nirbhar Bharat mission by sourcing seeds directly from Indian farmers, maximizing their income and supporting local farming ecosystems.

CAT Technique

Our oil is refined under a controlled atmosphere and temperature, preventing contact with external air. This preserves 100% of the oil's natural nutrients.

Non-GMO Certification

We process exclusively non-genetically modified crops, promising a naturally healthy lifestyle and exporting high-protein de-oiled cakes globally.

AGMARK & HACCP Certified

Fully automated manufacturing facilities untouched by human hands. Sourced with premium grade, virgin packaging material for maximum freshness.

Zero Added Chemicals

We strictly avoid harmful chemicals, synthetic coloring, or artificial preservatives, maintaining absolute natural freshness and high taste integrity.

50+ Years Trust

Serving generations since 1970. Built on foundations of honesty, safety, and rigorous testing across all processing departments.
