OUR STORY & LEGACY

Pioneers in high-purity oil processing since 1970, delivering health and taste in every drop.

FIVE DECADES OF TRUST

Empowering domestic farmers while feeding millions of healthy homes.

Founded in 1970, Kirti Group began its journey as a humble local agro-processing business. Today, it stands as one of India's prominent industrial titans in edible oil manufacturing and high-protein de-oiled cake production.

Headquartered in Latur, Maharashtra, Kirti Gold operates as a highly integrated agrobusiness. Over the years, the group has expanded its operations across 6 key states. Our massive network comprises more than 15,000 retail endpoints and over 1,000 regional distributors who deliver our trusted range of edible oils, chana dal, besan, and bottled mineral water.

Our leadership structure blends veteran agricultural experts with seasoned processing technicians who are dedicated to securing pure natural crops, support domestic crop systems, and promote organic cooking mediums.

Core Corporate Objective:

"To bridge the gap between hard-working Indian agriculturalists and health-conscious modern families by providing nutritious, high-grade organic products at honest values."
